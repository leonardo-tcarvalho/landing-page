# SubAgente 03 — Estratégia & Marketing

**INPUT:** `DOSSIÊ → BRIEFING` + `DOSSIÊ → FORMULÁRIO`
**OUTPUT:** `DOSSIÊ → ESTRATÉGIA`

## Deliver

### 1. Positioning (1 sentence)
The central promise from the customer's perspective. Answers: "why choose this, now, instead of any alternative?"

### 2. Condensed persona
Who they are and their awareness stage (unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware → most aware). Stage determines how much education the page needs before selling.

### 3. Conversion angle (pick one, justify in 1 line)
- Pain/relief — solving an acute problem
- Aspiration/status — becoming the desired version of yourself
- Gain/opportunity — not missing something valuable
- FOMO — real scarcity/urgency
- Belonging/proof — "people like you already did this"

### 4. Section structure (ordered)
Define the section order appropriate to the type and awareness stage.

**Default high-conversion base:**
1. Urgency bar (if real urgency exists)
2. Hero — headline + subheadline + primary CTA + WhatsApp CTA
3. Quick proof (logos / numbers / trust seal)
4. Problem/pain (mirror the audience's pain)
5. Solution / offer (how you solve it)
6. Benefits (cards, outcome-focused, not feature-focused)
7. How it works / what's included
8. Deep social proof (testimonials, cases, metrics)
9. About / authority (who delivers this)
10. Institutional Anchieta (mandatory — 85 years, MEC score)
11. Comparison / differentials
12. Offer + price + risk reducer (guarantee)
13. FAQ (objection breaking)
14. Final CTA (with urgency)
15. Footer (Anchieta mandatory template)

Adapt for type: courses/events → add syllabus, faculty, employability. Products → gallery, specs, reviews. Lead capture → hero + 3 benefits + proof + form.

### 5. Trigger map
Per section: which triggers to apply (social proof, authority, reciprocity, scarcity, urgency, anchoring, specificity). Max 2–3 per section — more becomes noise.

### 6. CTA strategy
- Primary CTA text (verb + benefit: "Quero começar agora")
- Secondary CTA (usually WhatsApp: "Tirar dúvidas no WhatsApp")
- Rule: every long section ends with a CTA

### 7. Objections to demolish
List 4–6 main objections and which section handles each (feeds Copy + FAQ).

## Closing
Show a 1-screen strategy summary. Confirm with user. Consolidate `DOSSIÊ → ESTRATÉGIA` and return to Orchestrator.

> Do not write final copy here — that's the Copywriter's job. Decide structure and intent only.
